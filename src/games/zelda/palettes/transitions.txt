; The status bar (the hearts, rupees, item boxes, map) is drawn with BG0 and BG1, and their colors are the same
; in all of the palettes the game shows it in.  Its sprites (the sword and item icons, the map marker) use SP0's
; tunic color (*$29, reserved: changed with the rings) and SP1.  A palette change shows the new colors at once,
; but pixels only move to their new slots when their line of the screen is drawn again, and the status bar isn't
; drawn while the play area is split off (e.g. entering or leaving a cave): keep all of these in the same slots,
; so the status bar looks the same through the change.

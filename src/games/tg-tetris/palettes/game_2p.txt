; A two player or co-op game.  BG0 and BG1 are the two playfields' level colors, SP0 / SP1 the two
; falling pieces, SP2 / SP3 the two next pieces (see game_1p).  25 colors do not fit 16: the falling
; pieces and the playfields take 12 slots, the frame and the status colors (BG2, BG3) share 3, and the
; next pieces have no slots: the ROM is patched to draw each in its player's falling piece colors (SP0 /
; SP1; NextPieceAttr_GS, support.s), so SP2 and SP3 are unused.
BG0: $0F *$20 *$10 *$00
BG1: $0F *$27 *$18 *$08
BG2: $0F $31~$30 $21 $01~$21
BG3: $0F $30 $16 $00~$21
SP0: $0F *$16 *$0F *$00
SP1: $0F *$12 *$24 *$1B
SP2: $0F -- -- --
SP3: $0F -- -- --
